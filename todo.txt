# SPECIAL CHILD & AUTISTIC CARE SCHOOL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - MODULES TODO

## 1. Enquiry, Screening & Diagnostic Assessment (Pre-Admission Pipeline) [NEW WORKFLOW]
- **Enquiry & Lead Intake:**
  - Parent inquiry registration (walk-in, phone, referral from pediatrician)
  - Chief complaints / parent concerns (speech delay, hyperactivity, eye contact, repetitive behaviors, sensory aversions)
  - Follow-up scheduling & status tracking (Inquiry Received, Assessment Scheduled, Assessment Completed, Admitted, Dropped)
- **Clinical & Diagnostic Assessment:**
  - Pre-admission evaluation by multidisciplinary team (Developmental Pediatrician, Clinical Psychologist, Speech Therapist, OT)
  - Diagnostic tools & scoring (CARS, ISAA, DSM-5 criteria, Vineland Social Maturity, Sensory Profile checklist)
  - Baseline skill evaluation (receptive/expressive language, motor coordination, self-help skills)
  - Diagnostic report generation with recommendations (Therapy only, Day Schooling, Shadow Teacher required)
- **Conversion to Formal Admission:**
  - One-click convert screened diagnostic profile into enrolled student record
  - Assign student ID/Roll No, classroom batch, primary special educator, and therapy plan

## 2. Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) [COMPLETED]
- Multi-role user authentication with strict route protection:
  - **1. Admin Login:** Full administrative control (`admin@specialcare.com`)
  - **2. Therapist Login:** Diagnostics, CARS assessment, IEP & session notes (`priya@specialcare.com`)
  - **3. Parent Login:** Dedicated access to Parent Progress Portal (`parent@specialcare.com`)
- Protected application routes: All patient, clinical, and student data requires active authentication.
- Single-click quick role switchers on the login page for rapid testing and demonstrations.
- Dynamic navigation bar: shows only relevant menus per authenticated role.

## 3. Student & Child Profiling Module (Specialized)
- Detailed intake profile & enrollment details (birth history, age, emergency contacts, authorized pickup persons)
- Diagnostic details & medical background (Autism severity level, ADHD, SPD, allergies, seizure history, medications)
- Sensory Profile:
  - Sensory sensitivities (hypersensitive/hyposensitive to light, sound, touch, textures)
  - Triggers and distress signals
  - Calming strategies, comfort items, and de-escalation techniques
  - Communication mode (verbal, non-verbal, PECS, AAC devices, sign language)

## 4. Individualized Education Program (IEP) & Goal Tracking
- Custom IEP creation for each child with target academic, developmental, and functional goals
- SMART goal setting categorized by domain:
  - Speech & Language
  - Occupational & Fine/Gross Motor Skills
  - Behavioral & Emotional Regulation
  - Social & Play Skills
  - Cognitive & Life Skills / Activities of Daily Living (ADL)
- Milestone tracking status (Not Started, In Progress, Mastered, Needs Revision, Regressed)
- Periodic IEP review cycles (Monthly/Quarterly/Annual) with educator and therapist sign-offs
- Printable IEP documentation & PDF progress reports for parents and doctors

## 5. Multi-Disciplinary Therapy & Clinical Services Module
- Specialist therapy management:
  - Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)
  - Speech & Language Therapy
  - Occupational Therapy (OT) & Sensory Integration
  - Physiotherapy (PT)
  - Music, Art, and Play Therapy
- Therapist appointment & therapy slot scheduling
- Therapy session logs with clinical notes (SOAP format: Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
- Therapist-to-child ratio tracking and therapist assignment

## 6. Behavioral Tracking & ABC Incident Log
- ABC Data Collection (Antecedent - Behavior - Consequence) for tracking challenging behaviors
- Meltdown & sensory overload logs (trigger, duration, environment, interventions used, outcome)
- Positive reinforcement tracker & token economy / reward system
- Behavior analytics & incident pattern heatmaps (identifying triggers by time, noise level, or activity)

## 7. Daily Care & Routine Communication Log (Daily Sheet)
- Meal & feeding log (intake percentage, food texture tolerance, specialized dietary restrictions)
- Toilet & hygiene training log (potty schedules, diaper changes, accidents, independent requests)
- Nap & sleep tracker (duration, restlessness, calming duration)
- Mood and energy level check-ins (hourly or session-based visual scale)
- Medication Administration Record (MAR):
  - Prescribed dosage, scheduled times, administering nurse/staff verification, guardian consent
